    About this experiment

    This is an original interaction study for a visual-first Asterfall, not a full second campaign. It tests route reachability, one crew's warning cue and one named cable failure. A received signal is not evidence that people can act. Response is scripted for the stated crew conditions; travel time, forecast quality, other faults and real-world safety are not modeled. Parts are game costs. Nothing is saved or sent; Classic saves are untouched.

    Cables: tap one island, then another along a dotted route. Repeat the pair to remove a cable. Beacons: select the tool, then a quay. Both tools also work with Tab and Enter. Undo restores the last edit. Less motion removes the signal animation.
