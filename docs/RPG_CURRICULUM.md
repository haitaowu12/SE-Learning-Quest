Processes recur rather than serving as a prescribed one-pass waterfall; an ordered story is not a mandated engineering life-cycle model.

### q01

**The Bell That Stayed Green — Lantern House.** Define the warning service’s purpose and boundary, then identify evidence needed to investigate a warning that did not lead to action.

- **purpose:** Orren will fund a repair once you can say what the service is for. What purpose do you put at the top of Sera’s ledger? Feedback distinguishes weak: Restore a dependable transmission service at the council tower; strong: Enable shore crews at inhabited quays to act on a storm warning; mixed: Bring every island storm precaution into the rebuilding programme.
- **first-evidence:** Where do you spend the one calm afternoon before the ferry routes close? Feedback distinguishes strong: Trace one warning from the tower record to the crew’s actions; mixed: Repeat the council’s lamp test to establish a comparable starting point; weak: Defer investigation until an island-wide survey can settle the boundary.

**Workbench: Where the Warning Ends (select).** Use this investigation boundary: the service starts when the watchkeeper releases an authenticated storm order and ends when shore crews can recognize it and start the prescribed response. Select every activity inside that delivery boundary. Forecast creation and seawall design remain outside, with their interfaces recorded.

**Evidence explained:** The repeaters carry the order, local signals present it, and the watch handover keeps someone responsible for receiving and acting on it. Forecasting supplies an input; seawalls mitigate a different consequence.

**Artifact:** Sera’s Boundary Sketch. Case record: the council tower transmitted; the returning ferry did not change course. Neither observation disproves the other. The selected mission and investigation scope are recorded in this quest’s decisions. Candidate delivery chain: authenticated order → relay path → recognizable quay signal → staffed response. External dependencies: weather forecasting, vessel decisions and harbour defences. Open question: which evidence connects the green tower lamp to the people at the shore?

**Revisits:** Initial mission framing. **Reused later by:** [q02](#q02), [q03](#q03), [q04](#q04), [q12](#q12)

**The Weight of a Quiet Shore — Reed Steps Trial Shelter.** Apply an explicit weighted benefit model, distinguish ranking from acceptance and identify uncertainty that could change the decision.

- **decision-rule:** What rule do you use to compare the four support kits? Feedback distinguishes mixed: Pick the kit with the highest reach score and use the other columns only for ties; strong: Use the agreed weighted sum and preserve score sources, assumptions and acceptance conditions; weak: Add the three scores without weights so every column counts equally.
- **uncertain-reach:** The Reed Kit’s reach score could be 6 rather than 8 in exposed loading bays. What commitment do you make next? Feedback distinguishes strong: Keep the reservation provisional and test the exposed-bay reach before ordering the full lot; weak: Award the full lot using the current point estimates to secure the shipment; mixed: Choose Copper on its steadier estimate and schedule a later review.

**Workbench: Make the Weights Visible (trade).** Using the current point estimates only, select the kit with the largest weighted benefit: 5 × reach + 3 × restoration margin + 2 × ease of support. Every score is a benefit on a 0–10 scale; a larger support score means less support burden. This comparison ranks the supplied estimates and does not certify compatibility or storm performance.

**Evidence explained:** Reed has the unique highest point-estimate total: 5×8 + 3×8 + 2×8 = 80. Copper totals 75, Glass 73 and Runner 75. Reed’s uncertain reach remains capable of reversing this nominal ranking.

**Artifact:** Reed Steps Decision Ledger. Comparison rule: reach weight 5; restoration margin weight 3; ease of support weight 2. All values are normalized benefits, higher is better. Point-estimate totals: Copper 75, Glass 73, Reed 80, Runner 75. Reed is the unique nominal leader; its reach range 6–8 produces totals 70–80, with a tie at reach 7. Mandatory compatibility and service acceptance remain separate. The chosen commitment and uncertainty response are recorded in this quest, together with the need to revisit the ranking when evidence or priorities change.
